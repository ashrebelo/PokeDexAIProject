# PokeDex AI Project Product Brief

## Product Overview
PokeDex AI Project is a local-only Pokédex application for browsing Pokémon, signing in with a personal account, and saving favorite Pokémon. The experience is centered on quick discovery, simple account-based personalization, and a clean way to return to a short list of saved favorites.

The app is designed for individual use on a local machine. Users should be able to explore Pokémon data, view detailed information for any Pokémon, and keep favorites associated with their own account.

## Product Goals
- Make it easy to discover Pokémon by browsing a complete catalog.
- Let users create a personal account and return to their saved favorites.
- Provide a focused, lightweight experience that feels fast and simple to use.
- Keep the product centered on viewing and saving Pokémon, not on social or competitive features.

## Users
- A casual Pokémon fan who wants to browse Pokémon and inspect details.
- A registered user who wants to save a personal list of favorite Pokémon.
- A returning user who wants their saved favorites preserved between sessions.

## Feature Definitions

### 1. Register an Account
Users can create a new account so they can use the app as an identified, personal experience.

Done means:
- A new user can create an account successfully.
- The app rejects invalid or incomplete sign-up attempts with clear feedback.
- A new account is ready to use immediately after registration.

### 2. Log In and Log Out
Users can sign in to access their saved favorites and personal app experience, and sign out when finished.

Done means:
- A registered user can log in successfully.
- Incorrect login attempts are rejected with clear feedback.
- A signed-in user can log out and return to a signed-out state.
- A returning user can access their own saved data after logging back in.

### 3. Browse Pokémon
Users can view a browsable list of Pokémon.

Done means:
- The app shows Pokémon entries in a way that is easy to scan.
- Users can move through the catalog without needing an account first.
- The browsing experience is reliable and supports discovery of available Pokémon.

### 4. View Pokémon Details
Users can open a Pokémon detail view to learn more about a selected Pokémon.

Done means:
- Selecting a Pokémon opens a dedicated detail view.
- The detail view presents useful information for that Pokémon in a readable format.
- Users can move from browsing to details and back without losing their place in the app.

### 5. Save Favorites
Signed-in users can save Pokémon as favorites tied to their own account.

Done means:
- A logged-in user can mark a Pokémon as a favorite.
- A favorite remains associated with that user’s account.
- A user can view their saved favorites later after returning to the app.
- One user’s favorites do not appear in another user’s account.

### 6. Manage Favorite List
Users can review the Pokémon they have saved and remove items they no longer want in their list.

Done means:
- A logged-in user can see their current favorites.
- A user can remove a favorite from their saved list.
- The list updates to reflect the change immediately from the user’s perspective.

## Content Source Expectations
The app should present Pokémon information that feels complete enough for browsing and decision-making. Users should be able to rely on the app to show the core identity of a Pokémon and the information needed to recognize it at a glance.

## Done for the Product
The product is done when a user can:
- Register an account.
- Log in and log out.
- Browse the Pokémon catalog.
- Open Pokémon detail pages.
- Save favorites to their own account.
- Return later and still see their saved favorites.

The product is not about advanced search, battle systems, social features, or public sharing. Those are outside the current product scope.
